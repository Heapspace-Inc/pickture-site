# Pickture privacy policy

Effective November 16, 2026.

Pickture does not collect, store or share any of your data. Everything it does with your photos happens on your iPhone.

## What Pickture reads

- **Your photo library**, with your permission. Pickture reads photos to find near-duplicates, judge sharpness and faces, and group photos into days and albums.
- All of this analysis runs on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device frameworks. Your photos are never uploaded.

## What stays on your iPhone

- The results of the analysis, such as scores and labels for each photo.
- Your choices: which photos you kept or tossed, and which suggestions you skipped.
- What Pickture learned from your choices, and album title examples.
- People you named, with the face data used to recognize them and the name, phone number or email you picked from Contacts for each.
- How much space you have freed, and how many free deletes you have used. The free-delete count is also kept in your iPhone's keychain, so it survives reinstalling Pickture. Pickture never sends either one anywhere.

This data lives in Pickture's own storage on your iPhone and is included in your normal iPhone backup. Deleting Pickture deletes it.

## What leaves your iPhone

Pickture has no server and sends nothing to its developer. These Apple services are used:

- **iCloud Photos**, to download a full-size photo from your own iCloud library when needed, on Wi-Fi.
- **Apple Maps**, to turn the location of a group of photos into a place name for an album title. Only the coordinates are sent, never the photos.
- **The App Store**, to process the one-time purchase. Pickture never sees your payment details.
- **Messages or the share sheet**, only when you tap Send or Share. You choose the recipient and see the message before it goes.

## No tracking

Pickture has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account. It does not use advertising identifiers.

## Deleting photos

Pickture only deletes photos you mark to toss, and iOS asks you to confirm every delete. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in Photos, where you can restore them for 30 days.

## Children

Pickture is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
