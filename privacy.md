# Pickture privacy policy

Effective November 16, 2026.

Pickture does not collect, store or share any of your data. Everything it does with your photos happens on your phone. This policy covers Pickture for iPhone and for Android. The sections below describe the iPhone app, and the Android section lists where the Android app differs.

## What Pickture reads

- **Your photo library**, with your permission. Pickture reads photos to find similar shots of the same moment, judge sharpness and faces, and group photos into days and albums.
- All of this analysis runs on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device frameworks. Your photos are never uploaded.

## What stays on your iPhone

- The results of the analysis, such as scores and labels for each photo.
- Your choices: which photos you kept or tossed, and which suggestions you skipped.
- What Pickture learned from your choices, and album title examples.
- People you named, with the face data used to recognize them and the name, phone number or email you picked from Contacts for each.
- How much space you have freed, and how many free deletes you have used. The free-delete count is also kept in your iPhone's keychain, so it survives reinstalling Pickture. Pickture never sends either one anywhere.

This data lives in Pickture's own storage on your iPhone and is included in your normal iPhone backup. Deleting Pickture deletes it.

## What leaves your iPhone

Pickture has no server and sends nothing to its developer. These Apple services are used:

- **iCloud Photos**, to download a full-size photo from your own iCloud library when needed, on Wi-Fi.
- **Apple Maps**, to turn the location of a group of photos into a place name for an album title. Only the coordinates are sent, never the photos.
- **The App Store**, to process the one-time purchase. Pickture never sees your payment details.
- **Messages or the share sheet**, only when you tap Send or Share. You choose the recipient and see the message before it goes.

## Pickture for Android

- **What it reads:** your photos and videos, with your permission. It looks for several shots of the same moment, large videos, and old WhatsApp images.
- **On-device analysis:** Pickture uses Google's ML Kit on your phone to find faces and read text in images. Your photos are never uploaded.
- **What ML Kit sends to Google:** ML Kit may send Google diagnostic data: your device model and Android version, Pickture's version, performance numbers and error codes, and a per-install identifier that does not identify you. It never sends your photos or what it found in them. Google Play services may also download the text recognition model.
- **What stays on your phone:** analysis results, the photos you kept, and how many free tosses you have used. Deleting Pickture deletes them.
- **Purchases:** Google Play processes the one-time purchase. Pickture never sees your payment details.
- **Deleting photos:** Pickture only moves photos and videos you mark to toss to the trash, and Android asks you to confirm. You can restore them from the trash for 30 days.

## No tracking

Pickture has no ads, no tracking and no account, and it does not use advertising identifiers. Pickture itself collects no analytics. On Android, Google's ML Kit sends Google the diagnostic data described in the Android section above.

## Deleting photos

Pickture only deletes photos you mark to toss, and iOS asks you to confirm every delete. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in Photos, where you can restore them for 30 days.

## Children

Pickture is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
