# Pickture support

To report a problem or ask a question, email [heapspace.app@gmail.com](mailto:heapspace.app@gmail.com). Include your iPhone model and iOS version.

## Common questions

**Why does iOS ask before deleting?**
iOS asks you to confirm every delete by any app. Pickture batches a whole day's tossed photos into one prompt.

**I tossed a photo by mistake.**
Open Photos, go to Recently Deleted, and recover it. Deleted photos stay there for 30 days.

**Pickture says "Nothing new".**
The first scan checks your newest photos first. Keep Pickture open on Wi-Fi, or leave your iPhone charging overnight, and days appear as the scan finishes.

**Why aren't some photos included?**
Pickture never suggests deleting favorites, hidden photos, photos in your own albums, or photos you chose to keep.

**Where are the albums Pickture creates?**
In Photos, inside a folder named "Pickture".

**How many photos can I toss for free?**
Your first 50 tosses are free. A toss deletes one photo or video, which goes to Recently Deleted for 30 days. Shrinking a video or turning a Live Photo into a still also uses a toss, because the original is deleted. After 50 free tosses, a one-time purchase unlocks Pickture for good. There is no subscription.

**Does deleting in Pickture free up iCloud space?**
Yes, if iCloud Photos is on. A photo deleted on your iPhone is deleted in iCloud too, once it leaves Recently Deleted. Free up in Pickture shows how much space you have freed.

**I back up with Google Photos or Amazon Photos. Does tossing delete my backup?**
No. Those apps keep their own copy, so a photo you toss in Pickture stays in Google Photos or Amazon Photos. To free space there too, delete it in that app.

**What does Shrink do to a video?**
It saves a 1080p copy of the video with the same date, place, favorite and albums, then asks iOS to delete the original. If anything goes wrong, or you cancel the prompt, both copies stay. Slow-motion, Cinematic, spatial and edited videos cannot be shrunk, because the copy would lose those effects.

**What happens when I turn a Live Photo into a still?**
Pickture saves the photo without its few seconds of video, checks the new photo, then asks iOS to delete the Live Photo. Edited Live Photos and ones with Loop, Bounce or Long Exposure are left alone.

**How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?**
Open Settings in Pickture and tap Restore purchase. Family Sharing members get the unlock too.

**Does Pickture upload my photos?**
No. See the [privacy policy](privacy.html).
